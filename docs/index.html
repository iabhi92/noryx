Your AI coding coach,
not your AI autopilot.

Noryx watches how you solve problems on LeetCode and other coding platforms, remembers how you learn, and coaches you with hints and questions — never the answer, unless you ask for it.

Built to make you better, not faster

Most AI coding tools optimize for the accepted answer. Noryx optimizes for whether you could solve the next one alone.

🧭

Tutor, not autocomplete

Asks questions, gives hints, points out edge cases. It only writes a full solution if you explicitly ask — and warns you first.

🧠

Learns how you learn

Tracks patterns across sessions: where you get stuck, what you repeat, which topics you're actually improving at.

🪶

Stays out of the way

Runs quietly alongside the site you already use. No new editor, no copy-pasting problems into a chat window.
