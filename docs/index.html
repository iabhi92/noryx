Say hi to Miko.

Built to make you better, not faster

Most AI coding tools optimize for the accepted answer. Meow Mentor optimizes for whether you could solve the next one alone.

🐾

Tutor, not autocomplete

Miko asks questions, gives hints, points out edge cases. It only writes a full solution if you explicitly ask, and warns you first.

🧶

Learns how you learn

Tracks patterns across sessions: where you get stuck, what you repeat, which topics you're actually improving at.

🐈

Stays out of the way

Runs quietly alongside the site you already use. No new editor, no copy-pasting problems into a chat window.

Works where you already code

No new platform to switch to: Meow Mentor runs alongside the judges you're already using.

Local-first, by design

Your sessions, code, and problem history stay in your browser's local storage on your machine. Nothing is uploaded anywhere by default. Read the full privacy policy.
